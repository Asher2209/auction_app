How it works

From the seller's binder to your collection

  1. 01

    The card is reviewed

    The seller submits photos and details. A MintLot admin checks identity, set, number, condition and counterfeit risk, then marks it Platform Verified and records it as a token in the seller's wallet.

  2. 02

    You bid live

    New bids appear for everyone instantly. A bid in the final minute adds two minutes, so the auction always ends on a fair last word.

  3. 03

    Pay and own it

    The winner pays through MetaMask; the payment and the card's token move in the same transaction, and the seller ships the card.
