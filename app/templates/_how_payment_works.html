How the blockchain is used for this item

The item itself is not on the blockchain. Its photos, description and the answers above are kept in the ChainBid database, and the highest bid and the winner are decided by the auction engine, not by a blockchain.

The blockchain is used for one thing: the payment, and only if the winner chooses to pay with test cryptocurrency.

  1. The seller links a wallet to their profile by signing a short message with it, which proves the wallet is theirs.
  2. When the auction ends, the winner connects their own wallet. The server works out the price in test ETH and fixes that quote.
  3. The winner approves one transaction in their wallet. It goes through a small contract that passes the money straight to the seller's wallet; the contract never holds funds.
  4. The contract writes a public record naming this auction number, the buyer, the seller and the amount.
  5. ChainBid then reads the blockchain itself and checks the network, contract, buyer, seller, auction, amount and confirmations. Only then is the item marked paid. Your browser's word is never enough.
  6. The invoice shows "Blockchain Verified" with the transaction hash, which anyone can look up on the test network's explorer.

This uses a test network with valueless test ETH. A simulated card, UPI or wallet payment never touches the blockchain.
