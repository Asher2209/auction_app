1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website saves in your browser. ChainBid uses only the cookies it needs to work, plus one that remembers your cookie choice. It sets no advertising, analytics or tracking cookies.

3. Other things stored in your browser

If you use the light/dark switch, your choice is saved in this browser's local storage under the name chainbid-theme. It is not a cookie, is never sent to the server, and you can clear it in your browser settings.

Nothing else is stored for tracking. Files from third-party content delivery networks (jsdelivr, cdnjs) are fetched when a page loads; those services may log your IP address, and your browser may cache the files. See the Privacy Policy.

4. Your choice

Because every cookie above is essential, the site works the same whichever option you pick. The choice is recorded so that, if optional cookies are ever added, none is set without your agreement. Essential only means just the cookies in the table. Accept all would also allow optional cookies in future; none exist today.
