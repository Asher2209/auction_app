2. What ChainBid is

ChainBid is an online auction platform built as an academic project. Sellers list items, buyers bid, the system closes auctions and names winners, and winners pay. Payments are simulated or made with valueless test cryptocurrency; no real money changes hands and no real goods are exchanged unless the operator states otherwise in writing and has put in place a legal and payment setup that this software does not include.

4. Selling

  • You may list only items you own and have the right to sell, described honestly, with photos you have the right to use.

  • Sellers must not bid on their own items; the system blocks this.

5. Bidding

  • A bid is a binding offer. You cannot withdraw it. The highest valid bid when the auction closes wins.
  • A bid must be higher than the current bid (the first bid at least the starting price).
  • Anti-sniping: a valid bid in the last 60 seconds extends the auction by 2 minutes.
  • The server's clock decides when an auction starts and ends. Times are shown in UTC.
  • Bid manipulation, fake bidding, automated abuse and attempts to get around limits are not allowed.

6. Paying

  • The winner should pay the winning bid. The platform does not currently set a payment deadline, but the operator may cancel an unpaid sale.
  • Simulated payments (card, UPI, wallet) only pretend to move money and may succeed, fail or stay pending for test purposes.
  • Cryptocurrency payments are made in test ETH from your own wallet, directly to the seller's wallet through a small contract. The amount is quoted at a fixed exchange rate and locked when you start. The server accepts the payment only after it has checked the transaction on the blockchain. Blockchain transactions cannot be reversed.
  • An invoice is created after a successful payment.
