2. What ChainBid is

ChainBid is an online auction platform for collectible trading cards, built as an academic project. Sellers submit cards, an administrator reviews them, buyers bid, the system closes auctions and names winners, and winners pay. Payments are simulated or made with valueless test cryptocurrency; no real money changes hands and no real goods are exchanged unless the operator states otherwise in writing and has put in place a legal and payment setup that this software does not include.

3. What "Platform Verified" means

A card marked Platform Verified has been reviewed by a ChainBid administrator, who checked the submitted details and images against a checklist. It is not a guarantee that the physical card is genuine, and it is not professional grading. Grading details and certification numbers are supplied by the seller. A card's blockchain token records its platform identity and who owns it; it does not prove the physical card is authentic.
