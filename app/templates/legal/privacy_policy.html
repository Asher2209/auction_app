3. Technical data and cookies

Your browser's IP address is used briefly, in memory, to limit repeated login attempts and other abuse; it is not saved in the database. The web server's own logs may contain IP addresses and requested addresses. We set only the cookies described in the Cookie Policy: all of them are needed for the site to work, and there are no advertising or tracking cookies.

4. Who else receives data

  • Content delivery networks (cdn.jsdelivr.net and cdnjs.cloudflare.com) serve the Bootstrap, Socket.IO and Chart.js files. Your browser contacts them directly, so they see your IP address and browser details.
  • An email service delivers messages if the operator has configured one. In development, emails are only written to a log.
  • Your wallet and a blockchain node are involved when you link a wallet, register a card or pay with cryptocurrency: the wallet software you choose (for example MetaMask) is run by its own provider, and the server reads the chain through the node the operator configured.
  • Administrators, and authorities where the law requires it.

We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

5. How long we keep it

Account, card, bid, payment and invoice records are kept while your account exists, and afterwards for as long as needed to keep auction, ownership and invoice history consistent (for example an invoice the buyer or seller may need). Feedback is kept until an administrator removes it. Cookies expire as stated in the Cookie Policy.

6. The blockchain

When a card is registered, sold or paid for with cryptocurrency, a transaction is written to a public ledger run by many independent computers. We cannot edit or delete it, and neither can you. It contains wallet addresses, a token number, an amount and a contract call, but not your name. Link only a wallet you are comfortable connecting to your cards and purchases.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Correct your name, phone and address yourself on the Profile page. You can also link a different wallet there, or remove it.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, or ask us to delete your account or stop using your data, by writing to {{ contact_email }}. There is no self-service export or deletion button yet, so an administrator handles these requests by hand.
  • We may have to keep some records (completed purchases, invoices, card ownership history) and cannot remove blockchain entries; we will tell you what was kept and why.
  • Cookies: you can change your choice at any time on the Cookie Policy page.
  • Depending on where you live you may also have the right to complain to a data-protection authority.

8. Security

Passwords are hashed; connections should run over HTTPS in production; forms are protected against cross-site request forgery; a login ends after 8 hours; failed logins are rate-limited; uploaded images are checked and renamed. No system is perfectly secure, and a demonstration installation should not be used for real personal data.

9. Children

ChainBid is for people aged 18 and over. Do not register if you are younger.

10. Changes

When this policy changes we update the version and date above. If the change is significant, the cookie banner will ask for your choice again.
