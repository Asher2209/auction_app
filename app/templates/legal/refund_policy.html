Refund Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

1. Auction Bids

All auction bids are final and non-refundable. By placing a bid, you agree that you intend to purchase the item at your bid price if you win the auction. If you believe an item is misrepresented, you must contact the seller within 48 hours of auction completion.

2. Item Not As Described

If you receive an item that is significantly different from the seller's description, you may request a refund within 7 days. You must provide photographic evidence of the discrepancy. ChainBid will investigate and make a determination.

3. Non-Delivery

If you do not receive your item within the agreed timeframe and after contacting the seller, you may open a dispute. ChainBid will investigate and may issue a refund at our discretion.

4. Seller Refunds

Sellers are responsible for refunding buyers who meet the criteria above. ChainBid can mediate disputes but does not guarantee refunds. In cases of fraud, ChainBid reserves the right to refund the buyer and pursue legal action against the seller.

5. Platform Fees

ChainBid platform fees are non-refundable once an auction has been completed. These fees cover the cost of operating the platform and processing transactions.

6. Cryptocurrency Transactions

Transactions made with cryptocurrency on the Sepolia test network are for testing purposes only. ChainBid is not liable for any losses related to cryptocurrency transactions. These transactions are irreversible.

7. Dispute Resolution Process

  1. Contact the other party directly to resolve the issue
  2. If unresolved, open a dispute on ChainBid with evidence
  3. ChainBid will investigate and make a determination within 7 days
  4. The determination is final and binding

8. Contact Us

For refund inquiries, please contact support@chainbid.test
