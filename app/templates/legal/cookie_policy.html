Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help us recognize you and remember your preferences. They allow us to provide personalized experiences and improve our services.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the platform to function. They include:

  • session - Manages user sessions and authentication
  • csrf_token - Protects against cross-site request forgery attacks
Preference Cookies

These cookies remember your preferences:

  • theme_preference - Stores your preferred light/dark mode
  • currency - Remembers your preferred currency display
Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how you use our platform:

  • _ga - Google Analytics tracking (if enabled)
  • page_views - Tracks page navigation
Marketing Cookies

These cookies support our marketing efforts:

  • utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign - Track marketing campaigns

3. Cookie Consent

When you first visit ChainBid, a cookie consent banner will appear. You can:

  • Accept All - Accept all cookies
  • Manage Preferences - Choose which types of cookies to accept
  • Reject All - Reject non-essential cookies

Essential cookies are always used to provide basic functionality. You can change your cookie preferences at any time in your account settings.

4. How to Disable Cookies

You can disable cookies through your browser settings:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy → Cookies and other site data
  • Firefox: Preferences → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Cookies and website data
  • Edge: Settings → Privacy → Cookies and other site data

Note: Disabling cookies may affect your ability to use certain features of ChainBid.

5. Third-Party Cookies

We may use third-party services that set their own cookies:

  • Google Analytics - Privacy Policy
  • Cloudflare - Privacy Policy

6. Cookie Retention

Session cookies are deleted when you close your browser. Persistent cookies are retained based on their purpose:

  • Authentication cookies: Until logout or session expiration (30 days)
  • Preference cookies: 1 year
  • Analytics cookies: 2 years

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about our use of cookies, please contact privacy@chainbid.test
